# SciPy attribution

The four-coordinate bounded trust-region solver in `_trf_solver.c` and
`_trf_trust.c` adapts algorithms and numerical helpers from SciPy 1.17.0,
`scipy.optimize._lsq.trf` and `scipy.optimize._lsq.common`.
